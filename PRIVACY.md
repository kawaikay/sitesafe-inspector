# SiteSafe Inspector Privacy Notice - Draft

SiteSafe Inspector is designed as an offline-first construction safety inspection tool. Inspection records, photographs, signatures, company branding and report data are stored locally on the user's device unless the user chooses to export or share them.

## Data handled
- Project and report reference information
- Inspector name, organisation and role, plus the works-responsible-party representative, organisation and role entered by the user
- Inspection selections, remarks and signatures
- Site photographs captured or selected by the user
- Optional company name, team name and company logo

## Sharing
The app does not intentionally transmit inspection data to a central service in this commercial MVP. Data leaves the device only when the user explicitly exports, prints or shares a report or image, subject to the destination service selected by the user.

## Retention
Saved inspections remain on the device until deleted by the user or removed with browser/app storage.
